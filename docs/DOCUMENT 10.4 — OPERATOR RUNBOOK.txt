# DOCUMENT 10.4 — OPERATOR RUNBOOK

**System:** Joint · **Licenses:** both · **Tier:** 10 · **Maturity:** InDevelopment · **© Maher**

The operational guide for those running deployments: provisioning (Laravel 13/PHP 8.3+, PostgreSQL, Redis, Octane/FrankenPHP, Horizon, Pulse), key custody (the threshold/M-of-N model from the Coercion-Resistance Spec — no single operator holds a dispositive key), the SBOM and reproducible-build pipeline, the network-source-availability obligation (MVL/AVL §B.6 — a modified deployment must offer its corresponding network source, with only minimal-and-justified redaction of keys/secrets), monitoring and the circuit-breaker controls, the crisis runbook integration (document 8.1), and the reconciliation job operation (alert on drift, never auto-correct). It states the operator's binding obligations under the licenses, including that operators custodying value must maintain crisis-disclosure timelines and proof-of-backing (M-§C.15). Honest caveat: the runbook must make unmistakably clear that an operator — even in emergency — cannot cross a constitutional invariant (there is no override of I6/I7 even for an incident commander), because the operator runbook is exactly where a "temporary emergency exception" would otherwise be quietly introduced.
