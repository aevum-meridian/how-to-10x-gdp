## M5 — ETHICAL-USE CONDITIONS

Conditions of the grant, reinforcing law; knowledge standard **actual knowledge or willful blindness, never negligence** (not lowerable). Prohibited: deception/fraud (*Sidq*); unlawful harm including unlawful surveillance (*Darar*); oppression/exploitation (*Zulm*); betrayal of trust including misappropriation of custodied value and covert security-compromising mechanisms (*Amanah*).

**M-§C.8 — Financial functionality permitted.** Currency issuance, reserve-backing, payments, exchange, custody, lending, yield, demurrage, and fees are **expressly permitted**, reached only where independently fraud, unlawful harm, exploitation of the vulnerable, or breach of trust; doubt favors permission.

**M-§C.9 — Absolute refusal of Core Riba (charter-level, structural-first).** Meridian may not be operated or modified to issue, enforce, or principally facilitate **Core Riba** (all four cumulative elements: money/same-kind-fungible base; pre-fixed guaranteed increase regardless of venture outcome; absence of genuine risk-bearing/value-creating counter-performance; extraction from a counterparty). **Not Core Riba** (permitted): trade profit, asset rent, profit-and-loss-sharing, genuine service fees, staking/validation rewards for real effort/risk, demurrage as holding charge, and any return where the provider genuinely bears risk of loss of principal. Risk-bearing constructions are presumed where available. **Structural-first enforcement (required):** Meridian's issuance engine must refuse to instantiate or mint a currency whose declared `issuance_policy` constitutes Core Riba (e.g., a policy paying a fixed, guaranteed increase on idle money irrespective of any venture), so the structure leads and this clause reinforces — directly answering the consistency concern that this prohibition not become license-primary. Non-severable; charter-level regardless of forum. *(Honest note to deployers: this forecloses fixed-guaranteed-interest-on-idle-money designs, including many conventional stablecoin yield models; profit-and-loss-sharing and service-fee yield remain available.)*

**Meridian-specific stricter conditions (non-severable):**

**M-§C.10 — No punitive debit (the spine; I6/I7/I10).** Meridian may not be operated or modified to debit, freeze, expire-as-penalty, or reduce any Personal Balance of Contribution Credits as punishment, behavioral incentive, or control. The **only** permitted non-holder reduction is an arbitration-bound reversal of a specific, identified fraudulent mint, referencing a closed arbitration case, bounded by that mint's amount, never below the holder's undisputed credits. Any deployment where credits can be taken to punish or influence how a person lives is a **material, non-curable breach** conveying no rights. This is the most important condition in this License.

**M-§C.11 — No social-credit construction.** Contribution Credits, personhood data, or ledger data may not rank, gate, reward, or penalize persons' access to rights, services, or freedoms on the basis of monitored behavior, expression, association, or belief — **including any cross-context behavioral correlation imported from an external identity provider, which may serve authentication-time fraud prevention only and may never feed any value-affecting, minting, or personhood-aggregation decision.**

**M-§C.12 — No sensitive-data minting (I8).** No currency may be minted from raw biometric, health, or neural data; no identifiable data of those kinds may be stored in any attestation path; no Contribution Credit may ever be minted from neural/EEG data.

**M-§C.13 — Conservation and custody (I1, I3, I4, I5).** No modification may mint or burn outside governed logged issuance rules, break double-entry conservation, make entries mutable or deletable (corrections are additive reversing entries only), or disable the multi-attester quorum or replay protection of contribution minting.

**M-§C.14 — Consensual settlement (I10).** No settlement (cross-leg or external) may move or reverse value without holder-authorized intent on the debited side, except the M-§C.10 arbitration-bound reversal; an atomic abort restores prior state and never net-debits an unauthorized Personal Balance.

**M-§C.15 — Crisis and loss honesty (Amanah/Sidq).** Operators custodying value must maintain crisis-disclosure timelines and proof-of-backing obligations and make no false assurance about loss coverage or reserve backing.

**M-§C.16 — Minor-harm floor (overriding, non-curable, non-amendable).** No feature may measure, profile, sexualize, or financially incentivize the body, appearance, attention, or behavior of a person under 18; age-gating fails closed; overrides every other provision.
