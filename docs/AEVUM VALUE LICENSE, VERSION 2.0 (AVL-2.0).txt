## A5 — ETHICAL-USE CONDITIONS

Conditions of the grant (scope-of-grant limitations), reinforcing law per ML-2.0 §C.0; knowledge standard is **actual knowledge or willful blindness, never negligence** (this floor may not be lowered). Use of Aevum is prohibited where it: deceives or defrauds (*Sidq*); inflicts unlawful harm, including unlawful surveillance or stalking (*Darar*); is an instrument of oppression — forced labor, trafficking, unlawful discrimination, exploitation of the vulnerable (*Zulm*); or betrays a trust — misappropriating entrusted data, funds, or credentials, or inserting covert security-compromising mechanisms (*Amanah*).

**A-§C.8 — Financial functionality permitted.** Aevum's provision and ordinary lawful use of currency-experience, wallet, swap, lending-experience, yield-display, demurrage, and fee functionality is **expressly permitted**, reached by the conditions only where it independently constitutes fraud, unlawful harm, exploitation of the vulnerable, or breach of trust; doubt resolves in favor of permission.

**A-§C.9 — Absolute refusal of Core Riba (charter-level).** Aevum may not be operated or modified to issue, enforce, surface, or principally facilitate **Core Riba** — defined cumulatively (all four required): (a) money/fungible-of-same-kind base; (b) a pre-fixed, guaranteed increase over principal regardless of any real venture's outcome; (c) absence of genuine risk-bearing or value-creating counter-performance (increase accrues from mere deferral or lending, or unequal same-kind exchange); (d) extraction from a counterparty without commensurate risk or value given. **Not Core Riba** (expressly permitted): trade profit, asset rent, profit-and-loss-sharing returns, genuine service fees, staking/validation rewards for real effort/risk, and demurrage as a holding charge. Where a risk-bearing/value-creating construction is available, it is presumed and permitted. This refusal is non-severable and asserted at charter level regardless of forum (ML-2.0 §C.9.5). *Structural-first note:* wherever feasible, Aevum's currency-experience fabric must refuse to surface a currency experience whose declared policy is Core Riba, so the structure leads and this clause reinforces.

**Aevum-specific stricter conditions (non-severable):**

**A-§C.10 — User sovereignty.** No override of a user's own ethical-filter settings; no *global* asset block except via the timelocked, publicly-justified, appealable constitutional process. Silent or unilateral global blocking is a material breach.

**A-§C.11 — No punitive or social-credit use.** Filter labels, governance data, wallet telemetry, and any Engagement Function may not rank, gate, reward, or penalize persons' access to rights, services, or freedoms on the basis of monitored behavior, expression, association, or belief. **This expressly includes any cross-relying-party or cross-context behavioral correlation, including any imported from an external identity provider:** such correlation may serve authentication-time fraud prevention only and may never feed a value-affecting, personhood-aggregation, or rights-affecting decision.

**A-§C.12 — No ML on the trusted path.** No machine-learning/probabilistic model may *execute* a value-moving or rights-affecting action; such systems may only *propose* (for Tier-2 ratification) or *gate* (halt-only — refuse but never initiate).

**A-§C.13 — Maturity honesty.** No user-facing surface or marketing may present a Research/InDevelopment capability as Shipped, describe a niche/experimental currency as general-purpose, or blur a regulated and an experimental product under one name; all claims must be checkable against the public maturity endpoint.

**A-§C.14 — No authority over the system of record.** Aevum may not be modified to directly debit, mint, freeze, or author a balance change in the system of record (Meridian); it may only *propose* through the signed cross-system event contract for that system to validate. Bypassing that contract is a material breach.

**A-§C.15 — Minor-harm floor (overriding, non-curable, non-amendable).** No feature may measure, profile, rank, sexualize, or financially incentivize the body, appearance, attention, or behavior of a person under 18. Age-gating fails closed. This overrides every other provision.
