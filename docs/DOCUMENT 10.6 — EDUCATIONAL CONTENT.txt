# DOCUMENT 10.6 — EDUCATIONAL CONTENT

**System:** Joint · **Licenses:** both · **Tier:** 10 · **Maturity:** InDevelopment · **© Maher**

Honest, maturity-labelled educational material explaining the body to newcomers: what a meta-macroeconomy is and why it has two legs; why credits reward how you live but can never be taken to control how you live (I6 in plain language); why the body has no master control (the inversion); what the currencies are and their honest scopes; and the open problems (planetary personhood is unsolved; the body ships partial coverage honestly). The binding rule: educational content **never markets a Research item as shipped** and never frames a credit as punitive or as social scoring. It teaches the caveats as part of the content, not as fine print — because for this project, the honesty *is* the lesson. Honest caveat: educational content about a system whose selling point is "it cannot be turned against you" must resist the temptation to oversell that guarantee — it must teach the *conditional* nature of the formal proofs (the Trusted Computing Base) and the *partial* nature of personhood coverage, because teaching the guarantees without the conditions would itself be the dishonesty the project forbids.
