# DOCUMENT 10.5 — GOVERNANCE PROPOSAL TEMPLATES & COMMUNITY FORUM CHARTER

**System:** Joint · **Licenses:** both · **Tier:** 10 · **Maturity:** Research · **© Maher**

Templates for governance proposals (parametric and constitutional), each requiring: the change, the tier (and thus the quorum/timelock), the attached simulation report (the blocking gate, document 9.1), the Trade-off Register impact, the maturity-label impact, and the public justification. The forum charter governs deliberation under the Foundational Principles: good-faith engagement (*Adl*), honest representation (*Sidq*), and — binding even on the Steward — the Humility Clause (a determination issued to suppress legitimate dissent is void). Constitutional proposals (touching the floors, I6/I7, child-safety) require the full process: personhood-weighted near-unanimity, long timelock, public justification, appeal window. Honest caveat: the templates must make the *hardest-to-change things visibly hardest* — a proposal touching a protection for persons must surface, prominently, that it faces the highest bar, so that the inverted difficulty ordering (protections-for-persons are hardest to weaken) is felt in the governance UX, not just stated in the constitution.
