# DOCUMENT 10.7 — MARKETING & BRAND GUIDELINES

**System:** Joint · **Licenses:** both · **Tier:** 10 · **Maturity:** InDevelopment · **© Maher**

Brand and marketing copy is bound, harder than any other surface, to two constitutional rules. First, the **maturity-binding rule** (AVL-2.0 §A-§C.13): no copy may describe a Research- or InDevelopment-tier capability as available; every status claim must be checkable against `/api/v1/maturity`. Second, the **naming-separation rule**: regulated and experimental products are never blurred ($PEG vs $PEG+ vs the index unit; $FLUX never called general-purpose; no contribution credit framed as punitive or as social scoring; no yield described in terms that imply Core Riba). Marketing may not represent the system as OSI/FSF "open source" (it is ethical-source — saying otherwise violates *Sidq*, ML-2.0 §0.2), and may not claim Islamic-finance compatibility without Shariah-board endorsement (claiming it without endorsement would itself risk *Gharar*). Honest caveat: marketing is, across every system in history, the surface where overclaiming originates, which is precisely why for this body it is bound *hardest* — the brand guidelines exist to make honest marketing the only compliant marketing, with every claim mechanically checkable against the maturity endpoint and the constitutional naming rules.
